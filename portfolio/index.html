My personal brand “PoloFighter” has a personal meaning but actually also a random one. I wanted to finally have my own nickname that does not sound like a ten-year-old made it, even though this one still sounds a bit like that. It is made up of “Polo”, which are the first 4 letters of my birth surname, and it is actually that half, which means that I split many things, I divide many things into halves, I know it like that, into halves. The second part actually has no meaning, I just thought it up while playing Idler game and making fighting games. The logo is actually a “literal” joining of the name in Gúla dopolyce and then only the “teeth” of the F. Now I have a second version which is cleaner, simpler and rounder, friendlier, minimalist, and the F was inverted in the logo. The colors also have no deeper meaning, because I like them and when I saw them, my eyes went straight to them. It is a style I like, it is my unique personal brand. Similarly, I have such a variation which is more [illegible], inspired by a building that helped me in difficult times.
